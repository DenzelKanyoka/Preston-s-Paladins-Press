Latest patch notes summary

A new champion named Nyx has been added. She is an off tank frontline. The payload gamemode has also returned but will only be available as a limited time mode taking place during the weekdays. The new battle pass has four skins. Tow for Saati and two for seris. As usuall there are also sprays and emotes as well as skin and battle boosters. Vivian, pip and koga each get a skin outside the battlepass this time. Pip, Furia, Grover and Grohk have had their healing buffed. Androxed has had one of his talents swapped. A lot of cooldowns were reduced and bugs were fixed.
