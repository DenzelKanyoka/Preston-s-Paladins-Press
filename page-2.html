Payload returns to Paladins after half a decade

After 5 of being removes, the payload gamemode return to paladins. However it is in a unique format. It will now be in a limited time mode that is available only during weekdays. The two maps have rturned unchanged but as a result, some newer champions such as yagorath, might not interact will with them as they were designed at a time were payload was thought to never return. Based on the reactions of all the popular content creators and their fan bases, it is safe to say that a majority are excited for the return of this mode.

Paladins Developer gets fired after a fight with a fan

After the announcement of the new payload gamemode Syberbolt, a famous youtuber of the game, had a heated confrontation with a developer. It was not pretty to say the least. They faught beacuse Syberbolt was angry that a developer wanted to remove the payload gamemode right around the time of its announcement. The developer was trolling and his conduct of the situation was unproffessional thus resulting in the termination of his employement.

New champion Nyx is the first new champion to not be overpowered on release

The new champion, Nyx, is heavily underpowered. Her dps and self sustain below the standards needed for her to be competetively viable against other champions in combat. This actually marks a first for the game as ever since the release of Vivian, every new champion released has allways needed emergency balance changes to tune them down as they were overpeowered. This picked with the release of Azaan who is by far the most overpowered champion released to date. It appears the company is learning from their mistakes. Hopefully Nyx will be appropriately buffed in the coming weeks
