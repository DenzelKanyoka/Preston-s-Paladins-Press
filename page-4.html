What is the purpose of this website?

We deliver news of course. The difference between our news and the news from the dev's is that the dev's mostly give us news about the changes they make to the game and the new things they plan to add. We go deeper. We search for drama between community members. We look at what champions actually win and what the meta actually entails. We provide news on all fronts. not just boring patch notes.

Where do we get our info?

YouTube, Twitter, Reddit, Discord and any website that has absolutely any scrap of content related to paladins. Of course not all information is true, so we look at multiple sources and provide all view points without adding any of our own inputs and biases. We simply report on what is happining and not what our opinions are.
